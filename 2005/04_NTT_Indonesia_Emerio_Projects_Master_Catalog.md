# NTT Indonesia / PT Emerio — Enterprise Project Case Studies Catalog (2007 – 2020)
*Compiled from the master project catalog (Google Drive: 1Vk1KRU_TUchy5hxpfJ9jG5dagYd7zptT)*

## 1. Bank BTPN — Big Data Lake & Customer 360 Analytics
- **Challenge:** Inability to cross-sell effectively; siloed customer transaction data across departments; lack of unified preference insights.
- **Solution:** Designed and delivered an enterprise Big Data lakehouse consolidating internal and external data sources in real-time, powering personalized recommendation engines and next-best-action models for the **Jenius** digital bank.
- **Outcome:** Tripled cross-sell conversion rates and established real-time behavioral churn prediction.

## 2. Bank Central Asia (BCA) — Regla® IFRS 9 ECL Automation
- **Challenge:** Indonesia's largest private bank required automated compliance with IFRS 9: classification via Business Model & SPPI cash flow testing, multi-stage Expected Credit Loss (ECL) modeling using PD/LGD/EAD forward-looking models, and Effective Interest Rate (EIR) amortization.
- **Solution:** Deployed Emerio’s proprietary **Regla IFRS 9** calculation engine integrating directly with BCA's core banking and data warehouses.
- **Outcome:** Benchmark delivery recognized for data precision, auditability, and on-time statutory submission.

## 3. Bank BTPN — Oracle General Ledger & Financial Accounting Hub (FAH)
- **Challenge:** Core banking system was unable to generate compliant accounting journals for non-standard transaction types; unstandardized Chart of Accounts (COA); severe reconciliation breaks between sub-ledger nominal balances and central GL balances.
- **Solution:** Deployed Oracle EGL and Financial Accounting Hub (FAH) with centralized COA rules and automated sub-ledger journal transformation.
- **Outcome:** Zero reconciliation breaks and accelerated financial month-end closing from weeks to hours.

## 4. Bank BTPN — Active-Active (DC-DR) Enterprise API Gateway
- **Challenge:** Slow API development, lack of standardization, and fragile integration impeding the rollout of BTPN Jenius digital banking; needed 99.99% availability.
- **Solution:** Implemented enterprise API Management with active-active DC-DR clustering, automated developer onboarding, and standardized RESTful microservices.
- **Outcome:** Reduced partner fintech integration time from months to days with 99.99% system uptime.

## 5. Bank BTPN — Back-Office Robotic Process Automation (RPA)
- **Challenge:** High volume of manual transaction entry combining batch and online actions across legacy systems, causing severe operational error rates and validation delays.
- **Solution:** Built automated bot workflows handling document verification, legacy screen entry, and multi-system cross-checks with human-in-the-loop exception queues.
- **Outcome:** 85% drop in transaction processing errors and significant operational cost reduction.

## 6. Bank Permata — Enterprise Data Warehouse Consolidation
- **Challenge:** Inability to analyze unstructured customer behavior; legacy data warehouse lacked scalability for big data analytics.
- **Solution:** Modernized warehouse architecture into a unified analytical data mart with streaming ingestion for real-time customer churn analysis.

## 7. Bank Permata — Partner API Management & H2H Modernization
- **Challenge:** High costs and protracted delivery cycles for corporate partner enablement over legacy leased lines (H2H).
- **Solution:** Deployed an API management gateway standardizing file communications and real-time transaction APIs for corporate clients.

## 8. Permodalan Nasional Madani (PNM) — Oracle Financials & Microfinance GL
- **Challenge:** State-owned microfinance giant needed a consolidated Chart of Accounts (COA) across 11 distinct corporate entities; 12 source interfaces with 6 file formats; complex multi-currency and Net Open Position (NOP) across hundreds of bank accounts and thousands of micro branches.
- **Solution:** End-to-end design and rollout of Oracle Financials and FAH tailored for nationwide micro-lending.

## 9. Bank Danamon — Multi-Channel Enterprise Transaction Reconciliation
- **Challenge:** Massive volumes of intraday transactions across ATM, Cash Deposit Machines (CDM), Visa, MasterCard, and bill payment channels causing overtime backlogs and missed settlement windows.
- **Solution:** Engineered automated multi-way reconciliation engine matching streams in near-real-time and surfacing genuine settlement breaks.

## 10. Bank Indonesia (Central Bank) — SWIFT Confirmation Matching
- **Challenge:** Operational exposure and manual latency when processing and confirming third-party high-value SWIFT transaction messages before final interbank settlement.
- **Solution:** Automated confirmation matching engine with automated SWIFT MT message ingestion and discrepancy escalation.

## 11. Indonesian Deposit Insurance Corporation (LPS) — Core Statutory System
- **Challenge:** National deposit insurance corporation required core computational infrastructure for deposit guarantee eligibility, bank risk assessment, and premium calculations.
- **Solution:** Built and delivered the core solution governing national deposit insurance calculations across all Indonesian commercial banks.

## 12. Bank Indonesia & OJK — Regla® Antesena Reporting Suite for 10+ Banks
- **Challenge:** Central Bank of Indonesia and OJK introduced stringent, granular reporting mandates (Antesena, LBU, LBBU, SLIK, LKPBU) with severe financial penalties for non-compliance.
- **Solution:** Engineered Emerio’s proprietary Regla Antesena regulatory engine and banking data model, adopted by 10+ major regional banks.
- **Outcome:** Two client banks achieved the regulator's prestigious **Best National Implementer** award.

## 13. Prudential Indonesia — BMC Control-M Batch Automation on iSeries
- **Challenge:** Nightly insurance batch processing on iSeries AS400 was executed manually by operators, prone to human error and SLA breaches.
- **Solution:** Configured end-to-end batch job dependencies and automation in BMC Control-M for daily, weekly, and monthly schedules.

## 14. BCA Finance — Mobile Customer & Dealer Financing Application
- **Challenge:** Needed mobile self-service for car installment simulation, contract early-settlement calculators, and vehicle registration (STNK) extensions.
- **Solution:** Native mobile application integrated directly with BCA Finance’s credit underwriting and loan servicing engines.

## 15. Astra Life (Astra Aviva) — Online Insurance Sales & Agent Portal
- **Challenge:** Rapidly roll out a direct-to-consumer digital insurance storefront with payment gateway integration.
- **Solution:** Built high-performance Angular-based portal with dynamic CMS, quotation engines, and payment gateway connectivity.

## 16. Top-Tier Indonesian Bank — 1,500-Branch Core Banking Migration
- **Challenge:** Migrating 1,500 nationwide branches from legacy mainframe core banking to modern Oracle infrastructure with zero business interruption.
- **Solution:** 4-year Managed Services leadership ensuring database clustering, dual-run data parity, and staged cutovers.
