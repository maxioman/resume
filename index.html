The approach

Five steps, in order. Most programmes start at step three. Starting at step one is what keeps the organisation in control afterwards.

  1. Assess oversight readiness

    Baseline how each function is delivered across systems A, B and C, how they hand off, and what is measured today, before any AI.

  2. Map the value stream

    Trace data, validations, approvals and execution from front office to general ledger and regulatory reporting. Redraw it as a target operating model.

  3. Wrap legacy building blocks

    Add AI extraction, validation and reconciliation to existing blocks, each with explicit exit criteria.

  4. Place oversight checkpoints

    Probes, guardrails and human-in-the-loop sanity checks keep tracking, SLA and control evidence continuous.

  5. Govern locally

    Authority, approval and SOP compliance stay with accountable local owners.
